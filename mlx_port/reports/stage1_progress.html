Live Decisions (do not regress)
  • Tokenizer IDs — add 4000 <iN> tokens, then specials. Assert against config.json (cot_start=155677, traj_future_start=155681, vocab=155697).
  • History fusion — DeltaTrajectoryTokenizerMLX: 16 × xyz = 48 tokens. Fuse pads 48→0, <iN> 0→48.
  • Vision grid — NVIDIA processor emits 16 × [1,H,W]. enforce_alpamayo_temporal_grouping is a port-only experiment; leave it off for greedy e2e and sample evals.
  • Conv3D — processor flats are C×T×H×W, 1536=3*2*16*16. AlpamayoPatchEmbed keeps NCHW + raw PT weight. Do not unflatten as (N, 2, 16, 16, 3).
  • RoPE — HF Qwen3-VL get_rope_index. Do not forward pixel_values into the stock LM after embeddings (wipes cached 3-row ids). Decode: arange(L)+cache_offset+rope_deltas. Sync cache._idx.
  • Generate stop — AlpamayoGenerateStop: immediate stop on Qwen EOS [151645, 151643] (<|im_end|>, <|endoftext|>); delayed stop-after-one on <|traj_future_start|>. Do not mask <|im_end|>. Fresh stopper per sample.
  • Sampling — NVIDIA test_inference.py is temperature=0.6, top_p=0.98, do_sample=True. Greedy e2e pins T=0 / top_p=1. “Top-k CoC” is first-token rank + greedy complete (diagnostic). Temperature eval is N independent full rollouts, not a ranked top-3.
  • Loader — maybe_stream=True even for the local SSD. physical_ai_av open_file only reads the HF cache, not the flat /Volumes/MicronSSD/pai_coc snapshot. Do not download NVIDIA’s example clip 030c760c-… (chunk 3119, not in CoC).
  • Diagnostics — verbose prefill / RoPE / step dumps only when ALPAMAYO_DEBUG=1.
